whats this TOF?

04:29 PM

Time-of-flight (TOF) is a range sensing technology that uses light or sound waves to measure the distance between the sensor and an object. In a TOF sensor, a light or sound pulse is emitted and the time it takes for the pulse to bounce back to the sensor is measured. By calculating the time it takes for the pulse to travel, the sensor can determine the distance to the object.

TOF sensors are commonly used in applications such as robotics, drones, and 3D scanning. They offer several advantages over other range sensing technologies, including high accuracy, low power consumption, and fast response times. Additionally, they can be used in a wide range of environments and lighting conditions.
